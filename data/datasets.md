
# Datasets for GADMs

Datasets provide the driving observations, sensor measurements, scene annotations, and multimodal information required to develop and evaluate Generative Autonomous Driving Models (GADMs). Unlike benchmarks, which define evaluation tasks and protocols, datasets primarily provide the underlying data for model training, validation, and testing.

| Dataset | Relevance |
| --- | --- |
| **nuScenes** | Multi-camera and LiDAR observations for driving scene understanding |
| **PandaSet** | Multi-sensor driving data for 3D reconstruction and scene modeling |
| **Occ3D** | 3D occupancy annotations for geometric world modeling |
| **Argoverse 2** | Multi-sensor scene perception and motion forecasting |
| **nuScenes-QA** | Multimodal question-answer annotations for driving scene reasoning |
| **WOMD** | Multi-agent motion forecasting and interaction modeling |
| **ONCE** | Large-scale autonomous driving scenes for 3D perception |
| **LingoQA** | Driving video question-answer data for semantic understanding and reasoning |
| **BDD100K** | Diverse driving videos and annotations for multimodal scene perception |

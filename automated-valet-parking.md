# Automated Valet Parking (AVP)

> Curated from *A Survey on Generative Autonomous Driving Models: Foundations, Taxonomy, and Convergence* (manuscript version, October 2026). Reference numbers correspond to the current paper PDF; official publication/code URLs have not yet been independently verified.

AVP is treated as the last-mile extension of driving and a demanding test for integration in confined geometry.

## Representative studies
| Method | Focus | Ref. |
|---|---|---|
| ParkDiffusion | Heterogeneous multi-agent parking motion prediction | [74] |
| ParkDiffusion++ | Ego-intention-conditioned interaction prediction | [81] |
| V2X-VLM | Infrastructure and vehicle observation fusion relevant to occlusion | [99] |

## Evaluation considerations
Parking success rate, final position/orientation error, clearance, maneuver count, completion time, safety interventions, and performance under occlusion and reversing.
